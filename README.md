My idea reimagines Indian road and transportation infrastructure as a multi-layered, AI-governed system built directly in response to the specific failures of Indian roads today — chronic traffic congestion caused by outdated signal-based intersections that force vehicles to halt rather than flow, chaotic and unplanned junctions that lead to some of the highest accident rates in the world, poor or broken footpaths that push pedestrians onto vehicle lanes, absent or overflowing waste bins that turn markets and roadsides into dumping grounds, open or badly designed sewage systems that contaminate water and worsen road decay, and rivers turned into dumping sites because there is no real deterrent against civic waste disposal; it addresses each of these by proposing a three-tier road network — underground tunnels for heavy and express traffic, elevated flyovers for fast inter-zone movement, and surface roads for local and pedestrian use — connected through roundabouts and grade separation instead of traffic lights, following the proven signal-free model already working in Kota, Rajasthan, so that vehicles are guided through the city continuously instead of stopping at every junction;
